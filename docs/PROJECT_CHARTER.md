# Project charter

## Objective
Create a Finnish SPICT-4ALL 2026 translation with an evidence trail strong enough for expert scrutiny and submission to the SPICT International Programme / University of St Andrews.

## Design principles
- Source fidelity before fluency.
- Plain Finnish without loss of clinical meaning.
- Independent model diversity to reduce correlated errors.
- Human reviewers decide; AI proposes and audits.
- Every decision is attributable and reversible.
- The canonical English source is immutable.

## Definition of done for a submission candidate
1. All source units accounted for.
2. Two independent forward translations completed.
3. Synthesis completed with disagreement log.
4. Blind back-translation completed.
5. Semantic, clinical, terminology and plain-language critiques completed.
6. Every unit human-reviewed and explicitly approved/revised/rejected.
7. Final Finnish text inserted into a COPY of the official Word template without changing the English source text.
8. DOCX rendered and visually compared against the official layout.
9. Submission pack contains methodology, provenance, decision log, reviewer record and final document.
