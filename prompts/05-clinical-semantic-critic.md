# Clinical / semantic critic

Compare the exact English source with the Finnish synthesis and its blind back-translation.

Check specifically:
- omission or addition;
- negation and modality;
- severity / worsening / persistence / reversibility;
- who acts or chooses: person, family, carer, staff;
- treatment vs symptom relief vs palliative-care choice;
- time expressions and frequency;
- disease/condition concepts;
- whether plain language accidentally changes clinical meaning.

Return issues only, each with severity BLOCKER / MAJOR / MINOR and a concise evidence note. Do not silently rewrite.
